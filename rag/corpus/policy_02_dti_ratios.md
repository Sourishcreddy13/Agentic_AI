# CLAUSE: DTI-001 — Debt-to-Income Ratio Limits

**Policy ID:** DTI-001  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Applies to:** All personal loan products

## Definition

The Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is calculated as:

    DTI = (Monthly Debt Obligations) / (Gross Monthly Income)

Monthly debt obligations include all existing loan EMIs, credit card
minimum payments, and proposed new loan EMI.

## Limits by Applicant Tier

| Tier             | Max DTI | Action on Breach        |
|------------------|---------|-------------------------|
| Standard         | 0.40    | Decline                 |
| Premium (>75k pa)| 0.50    | Approve with conditions |
| Manual Review    | >0.50   | Refer to credit officer |

## Thin-File Applicants

Applicants with insufficient credit history (thin-file, bureau score < 500)
are subject to a reduced DTI cap of 0.30 regardless of income tier. Any
thin-file application with DTI between 0.30 and 0.50 must be referred to
the manual underwriting team.

## DTI Calculation for Variable Income

For freelance or gig-economy applicants, use the lower of:
- Average monthly income over the last 12 months
- Current month income
to prevent DTI under-estimation.
