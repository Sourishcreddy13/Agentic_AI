# CLAUSE: KYC-001 — Know Your Customer Requirements

**Policy ID:** KYC-001  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Applies to:** All loan products; mandatory before any credit assessment

## Mandatory KYC Checks

All loan applications must pass the following checks before credit
assessment may begin:

1. **Identity Verification** — Government-issued photo ID matched to
   application name and date of birth.
2. **Watchlist Screening** — Applicant name and identifiers screened
   against domestic and international sanctions and PEP lists.
3. **Income Plausibility Check** — Declared income cross-referenced
   against employment type and submitted payslips or ITR.

## Outcomes

| Outcome       | Condition                                  | Next Step              |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| KYC Pass      | All 3 checks clear, no flags               | Proceed to credit      |
| Manual Review | Income plausibility flag or minor mismatch | Compliance officer     |
| KYC Fail      | Watchlist hit or identity mismatch         | Application terminated |

## KYC Fail — No Further Processing

Applications that receive a KYC Fail outcome must not proceed to credit
assessment under any circumstances. A referral note must be issued to
the compliance team and a decline letter dispatched to the applicant.

## Data Handling

All KYC documents are retained for 7 years per regulatory requirements.
No KYC check results may be included in plain-text logs.
