# CLAUSE: PRICE-001 — Loan Pricing and APR Tiers

**Policy ID:** PRICE-001  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Applies to:** All approved personal loan applications

## Standard APR Tiers by Bureau Score

| Bureau Score Range | Income Tier   | Standard APR | Max Principal  | Max Term |
|--------------------|---------------|--------------|----------------|----------|
| 750 – 900          | Any           | 8.5%         | INR 5,000,000  | 60 months|
| 700 – 749          | >75k pa       | 9.5%         | INR 3,000,000  | 48 months|
| 700 – 749          | ≤75k pa       | 10.5%        | INR 1,500,000  | 36 months|
| 650 – 699          | Any           | 12.0%        | INR 750,000    | 36 months|
| 600 – 649          | Any           | 13.5%        | INR 500,000    | 24 months|
| 500 – 599          | Manual review | Manual review| Manual review  | Manual   |
| <500               | Thin-file     | See THIN-001  | See THIN-001   | See THIN |

## Conditions Appended to All Offers

All indicative offers must state:
1. The offer is indicative and subject to final underwriter sign-off.
2. The APR quoted is the representative rate; actual rate is confirmed on
   final approval and may vary by ±0.5%.
3. The offer expires 30 days from the date of issue.

## Over-Limit Exceptions

Any application requesting a principal above the tier maximum requires
sign-off from a Level-2 Credit Officer before an offer can be issued.
