# CLAUSE: THIN-001 — Thin-File Applicant Handling

**Policy ID:** THIN-001  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Applies to:** Personal loan applicants with bureau score below 500 or
                fewer than 3 active credit accounts in the past 24 months

## Definition of Thin File

An applicant is classified as thin-file if any of the following apply:

- Bureau score below 500
- Fewer than 2 open credit accounts with a payment history
- No credit history on record (new-to-credit)

## Mandatory Routing

All thin-file applicants must be referred to the manual underwriting team.
Automated approval is not permitted for thin-file cases regardless of
declared income.

## Manual Underwriting Assessment

The manual underwriting team will consider:
- Alternative data: rental payment history, utility payments, bank
  statement cash-flow analysis
- Employment stability: length of tenure, employer type
- Guarantor option: a co-applicant with a bureau score above 650 may
  substitute for the thin-file applicant's credit history

## Offer Constraints for Thin-File Approvals

If the manual underwriting team approves a thin-file application, the
following constraints apply:
- Maximum principal: INR 100,000
- Maximum term: 24 months
- Minimum APR: 14% (risk premium)
- Mandatory 6-month review clause

## Decline Conditions

Thin-file applicants are declined (without manual review) only if:
- KYC has already failed, OR
- DTI ratio exceeds 0.60
