# CLAUSE: ELIGIBILITY-001 — Minimum Eligibility Criteria

**Policy ID:** ELIG-001  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Applies to:** All personal loan products

## Income Requirements

Applicants must demonstrate a minimum declared annual income of INR 25,000
to be considered for any personal loan product offered by the bank.
Income below this threshold results in automatic referral to a specialist
officer for manual assessment.

Applicants with declared annual income above INR 75,000 are eligible for
the Premium Lending Tier, which carries preferential APR rates.

## Age Requirements

Applicants must be aged 21 years or older at time of application, and must
not exceed 65 years of age at the projected end of the loan term.

## Residency

Applicants must be domestic residents or hold a valid long-term visa with
at least 12 months remaining validity.

## Employment

The bank accepts applications from salaried employees, self-employed
professionals, and registered business owners. Gig-economy and freelance
income requires a minimum 12-month earnings history to be considered.
